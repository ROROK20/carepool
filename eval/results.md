How to read this table:

- **Kept quotes verbatim** is 100% by construction: the checker deletes any sentence whose quote is not word-for-word in its label section. The column shows it anyway, re-checked here against the raw openFDA field in the cache file (joined, whitespace collapsed) rather than the app's own parsed copy.
- **Byte-exact in raw field** is stricter: the quote appears in the raw field with no whitespace change at all. A gap between the two columns means a quote spans a line break or double space in the original label.
- **Failed the first check** counts sentences the writer got wrong on its first try; each one was sent back once with the checker's reason. QUOTE_NOT_FOUND means the quote was paraphrased instead of copied; DOSE_MENTION means the sentence or its quote carried a dose amount or a dosing frequency, which cards never show; WRONG_PART means the section cannot support that part of the card. **Removed** sentences never reach the family.
- **What the checker cannot see:** whether each plain-language sentence means the same thing as its quote. The checker proves the quote is real and in the right section; Part 4 is a first, model-judged measure of meaning, and the app always shows the quote next to the sentence so a reader can compare.
